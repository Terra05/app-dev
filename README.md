##Suits

*Suits is a legal drama about a brilliant college dropout, Mike Ross, who is hired as an associate by top corporate lawyer Harvey Specter at a New York City law firm, despite never having gone to law school. The show's central premise is the two men trying to win cases and maintain their secret from the rest of the firm, while navigating office politics and the challenges that stem from Mike's deception. The series also focuses on the personal relationships of the other lawyers and staff, including Louis Litt, Rachel Zane, and Donna Paulsen*
